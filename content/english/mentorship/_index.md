We are excited to launch the **2026/2027 cycle of the MIUS Mentorship Program!**

This year, we are introducing a few exciting updates. Going forward, our open discussion sessions will be **accessible to all trainees and research associates,** whether you formally sign up as a program participant or just want to drop in. 

Why Peer Mentorship? 
Mentorship means something different to everyone, but building strong relationships takes time. Our goal is to help you build long-term connections enabling you to navigate:
  **Academia:** Connect with peers who can offer practical insights on building academic careers
  **Networking:** Expand your professional community across departments.
  **Career Transitions:** Learn from peers who have recently navigated job searches, industry transitions, or higher degree pathways.

**What to Expect:**
We will host regular, in-person discussion sessions tackling common challenges trainees face, creating a welcoming space to connect and exchange ideas.
