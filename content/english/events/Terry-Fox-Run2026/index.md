A reminder that you can still register for the Terry Fox Run / Walk up until the day of the run, September 20th at Stanley Park! There is a 3k walk or a 10k run and no cost to register. All are all encouraged to join! Register with Team Vancouver Prostate Centre [here](https://run.terryfox.ca/team/vancouverprostatecentre)!

The Trainee Committee will be hosting a bake sale on Thursday, September 17th. All donations will be donated to the Terry Fox Foundation. Please consider bringing a sweet or savory treat for your colleagues! You can sign up [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdMXCCy5p6irYRwAj3kQnj7qQ_gVW2gHDqoWF3YK6a-IBlFmQ/viewform)!
